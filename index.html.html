About

Saksham Parmar is an Advocate practising before the Delhi High Court and the Dwarka Court in New Delhi. He handles criminal, civil, family, writ, cheque bounce and consumer matters, along with legal notices and drafting.

With chambers at both courts, he is easy to reach for clients across Delhi and the NCR. His work is guided by three principles: Justice, Law and Advocacy. That means careful preparation of every file, plain and honest advice about the options and risks, and steady communication from the first meeting to the final hearing.

Every matter is treated with the seriousness it deserves, and every client’s concerns are heard with patience and confidentiality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a consultation?

Call 8287791081, message on WhatsApp or email parmarsaksham4@gmail.com to request an appointment at either chamber.

Which documents should I bring?

Bring whatever you have: notices, summons, FIR copy, court orders, agreements, ID proof and a short timeline of events. Incomplete papers are fine for a first meeting.

Is my first conversation confidential?

Advocates are bound by a professional duty of confidentiality. However, please avoid sending sensitive details through this website, email or messaging apps before an advocate–client relationship is established.

Which courts do you appear in?

The Delhi High Court and the Dwarka Court, New Delhi. Please ask the chamber about any other forum.

Can you guarantee the outcome of my case?

No. Outcomes depend on the facts, the evidence and the decision of the court, and no advocate can promise a result. What you can expect is honest advice and careful preparation.

What are your fees?

Fees are discussed at the time of consultation, based on the nature of the matter.
